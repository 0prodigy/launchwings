# Pricing

> Source of truth for plan structure. UI, onboarding, Stripe products,
> and tier-gating in `apps/api` derive from this doc.

## Tiers

Three flat-monthly tiers, AI bundled. No per-message charges, no per-contact
metering, no AI add-on, no surprise overages. Founder buys a tier; the tier
covers everything inside the published cap.

| Tier | Price | Followers | Brands | AI replies / mo | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | **$79 / mo** | up to 25,000 | 1 | 5,000 | Indie streetwear / capsule fashion shipping their first 4-12 drops |
| **Growth** | **$149 / mo** | up to 100,000 | 1 | 25,000 | Established brand with 1+ year of drop history and a real DM volume problem |
| **Scale** | **$249 / mo** | unlimited | up to 3 | unlimited | Multi-brand operator (e.g., parent label + capsule sublabel) |

All tiers include: Brand-Voice Engine, Launch Playbook, IG + FB Page DM
+ comment engagement, Shopify connector, Hot-Lead Inbox, Launch
Dashboard, 14-day trial, monthly billing.

Growth adds: multi-language brand voice (English, Spanish, Portuguese),
priority email support (< 4h business-hour reply), advanced analytics
export.

Scale adds: up to 3 separate brand corpuses on one account, 1:1
onboarding call with a human, shared Slack channel for support, custom
launch-playbook template authoring.

## Soft-cap behavior

When a tenant exceeds their monthly AI-reply cap:
1. At 80% — banner in dashboard with current usage + upgrade CTA.
2. At 100% — every subsequent reply is generated by Claude Haiku 4.5
   instead of Opus 4.7 for first-of-thread (quality drops but service
   continues). Banner: "You've hit your reply cap; we're using a faster
   model until your cycle resets. Upgrade to keep Opus on every first
   message."
3. No hard cutoff. No surprise overage charge. Manychat's bleeding wound
   is pricing surprise; we close that wound.

## What is NOT in any tier

- TikTok Shop integration (year 2)
- WhatsApp Business (year 2 / LATAM)
- Email / SMS channels (we don't compete with Klaviyo or Postscript)
- Native iOS / Android app (PWA only in v1)
- SSO / SAML (post-v1)
- Custom contracts / enterprise terms (no talk-to-sales until $30K MRR)

## Free trial

14 days, full feature access, credit card on file from signup. After 14
days, the card is charged the tier price. Cancel anytime; immediate
downgrade to read-only on next billing cycle.

## Design-partner pricing (Wave 0)

For the first 10 paying customers in Phase 1 (months 0-3):

- $39/mo for 12 months, then sunset to $79/mo (Starter) at month 13.
- Locked-in pricing is per the design-partner agreement, NOT lifetime —
  lifetime grandfathering is a Series A diligence flag we don't want.
- Each design partner gets weekly founder time (us) in exchange for
  feedback + permission to publish their results as case studies.
- Hard cap: 10 design partners. No exceptions.

## Why not metered or take-rate?

Considered and rejected:
- **Contact-count metering (Manychat's model):** Caused the 2026-03 user
  revolt. Bills surprise SMB founders. Killed.
- **Per-message AI charging (HighLevel's $0.02/msg):** Same trauma.
  Killed.
- **AI add-on (Manychat's $29 SKU):** Most-cancelled SKU in their stack.
  Killed.
- **Outcome-aligned / take-rate on attributed revenue:** Operationally
  fragile (DM-to-checkout attribution has match-failure modes), Stripe
  Connect Express adds 2-4 week onboarding tax, and SMB founders are
  trauma-anchored on revenue-share. Killed for v1; can revisit at year 2
  for a high-volume tier.
- **Usage-based seats:** Doesn't map to a one-person streetwear brand.

Flat tier with predictable cap is what the customer wants. We trust the
caps + Opus/Haiku routing to keep margin healthy.

## Unit economics (per the charter D4)

Inference cost per AI reply at Anthropic 2026 public pricing:
- Haiku 4.5: ~$1/M input, ~$5/M output → ~500 in + 200 out ≈ **$0.001 / reply**
- Opus 4.7: ~$3/M input, ~$15/M output → ~500 in + 200 out ≈ **$0.0045 / reply**

Realistic Starter customer per month (5K replies, 4 launches):

| Cost line | Amount |
|---|---|
| First-of-thread Opus replies (~1K) | $4.50 |
| Follow-up + classification Haiku replies (~4K) | $4.00 |
| Brand-voice ingestion (one-time per month re-embed) | $2.00 |
| Tone-card refresh (Opus, daily) | $3.00 |
| Launch playbook generation (4 launches × 6 beats × Opus) | $4.00 |
| Hot-lead summaries (Haiku) | $1.00 |
| Stripe fees (~2.9% + $0.30) | $2.80 |
| Hosting / DB / Trigger / Vercel allocation | $5-10 |
| **Total COGS** | **$26-31 / mo** |
| **Gross margin on Starter at $79** | **$48-53 / mo (60-67%)** |

Growth ($149) and Scale ($249) carry proportionally higher margin
because the fixed-cost-per-tenant amortizes.

## When pricing changes

Hard rules:
1. Existing customers grandfathered for 12 months on any tier increase.
2. Cap reductions never apply retroactively.
3. Price changes are announced 60 days in advance.
4. Communication is in-product banner + email, never a quiet TOS edit.

Why: avoiding Manychat's March 2026 pricing-trauma migration window —
which is, ironically, the window we're going to exploit on our way in.
