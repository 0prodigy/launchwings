# Vision

## The problem

It's 2026. The cost of *making* a product collapsed years ago — Shopify is
free for two weeks, AI builds your store page, and a streetwear brand can
go from sketch to live in a weekend. The cost of *launching* one didn't
collapse. It moved to Instagram.

A founder who runs a small streetwear or capsule-fashion label has a
better product than 90% of the brands that existed when DTC was new. She
also has, herself, the entire labor pool of her own business. Drop day, she:

- writes the launch caption
- shoots a teaser reel
- replies to every "do you have my size" DM
- chases shipping questions
- answers "when is the restock" 200 times
- watches comments for hot leads
- pushes urgency at hour-12 and hour-36
- recaps the drop in stories
- and somehow ships the orders

Funded brands hire a community manager + a content marketer + an ops
coordinator + a customer support lead to do this. Solo founders have one
human, a feed of AI tools that produce captions nobody clicks, and a
Manychat flow they built three months ago that hasn't been updated.

The bottleneck for IG-native brands is no longer building. It's running
the drop in a voice that sounds like the brand, at the speed Instagram
demands, without the founder collapsing.

## What LaunchWings is

**LaunchWings is the AI launch concierge for Instagram + Facebook brands.
We run the drop in your voice — captions, comments, DMs, and follow-up —
end-to-end, on autopilot.**

The product is six features that work together:

1. **Brand-Voice Engine** — ingests your last 100-200 IG captions, product
   copy, and past customer-DM threads. Every reply we send is grounded in
   your corpus. Every edit you make on a generated draft is captured and
   improves retrieval next time. The flywheel is the accumulated edit
   history.
2. **Launch Playbook** — pre-built sequences (Drop, Restock, Capsule
   Launch, Flash Sale, Pre-order), each a multi-beat timeline from tease
   to recap. Every beat is drafted in your voice; you approve before send.
3. **DM + Comment Engagement (IG + FB)** — Meta-Graph-API-native.
   Comment-to-DM funnel. Strict 24-hour-window compliance. Never bulk DM.
4. **Shopify-Native Connector** — order status, shipping, abandoned cart,
   restock notifications, all in your voice through IG/FB DM.
5. **Hot-Lead Inbox** — high-intent DMs surface to you for personal
   reply. The AI does the boring; you close the warm.
6. **Launch Dashboard** — mobile-first PWA. During a drop: posts going
   out, DMs flowing in, hot leads queued, revenue attributed live.

Where it lives:

- A mobile-first PWA dashboard (the founder runs her business on her phone).
- A native Shopify App, distributed through the App Store.
- A future Meta Tech Partner integration that gives us API priority.

LaunchWings is AI-native. We use Claude Opus 4.7 for brand-voice work and
Claude Haiku 4.5 for high-volume routing. Inference is bundled in every
plan — no add-on, no per-message charge, no surprise bill.

## Who we serve

**Primary day-1 ICP:** Independent streetwear and capsule-fashion brands
on Instagram (+ Facebook Page) with 5k-50k followers doing $100K-$2M/yr in
revenue, on Shopify or Etsy, that run drops / restocks / capsule launches
at least monthly. US + UK + AU first.

**Phase 1.5 expansion (month 4-6):** Beauty, accessories, jewelry,
supplement brands with the same revenue and drop-cadence profile.

**Year 2 expansion (month 12-24):** TikTok Shop integration. WhatsApp
Business. LATAM (Brazil + Spain, where Manychat AI is materially weaker).

**Anti-ICP (hard-refused at signup):**
- B2B SaaS, dev tools, AI products → wrong channel mix.
- Local service businesses (salons, dentists, gyms) → HighLevel territory.
- Enterprise / multi-brand agencies → not self-serve, wrong cadence.
- Regulated categories (CBD, supplements with health claims, firearms,
  adult) → Meta policy minefield, not worth the platform risk year one.

## Why it works

A streetwear founder opens LaunchWings the morning of a drop because over
the next 72 hours, the AI will write the launch caption in her voice,
answer the 800 sizing DMs, run the comment-to-DM funnel, surface the 12
hot leads worth her personal reply, push urgency at hour-12, and write
the recap. She does in three hours what would have taken her three
sleepless days, and the brand sounds like her — not like Manychat.

Across 30 days, she stops dreading drop day. Across 90 days, her edits
on our drafts get fewer because the corpus has learned her. Across a
year, she has fired the contractor she was paying $1,500/mo for community
management, and pays us $149.

The compounding asset is **each customer's accumulated edit history**.
Manychat can copy our features in six months. They cannot copy your
months of edits. That is the moat — and it lives inside each tenant, not
across them, so we never need a cohort to deliver value on day one.

## How we get paid

Three flat-monthly tiers, AI bundled, predictable bill:

- **Starter** $79/mo — 1 brand, up to 25k followers, 5K AI replies/mo
- **Growth** $149/mo — 1 brand, up to 100k followers, 25K AI replies/mo,
  Hot-Lead Inbox, multi-language, priority support
- **Scale** $249/mo — up to 3 brands, unlimited followers, unlimited AI
  replies, advanced analytics, 1:1 onboarding

14-day free trial. Credit card up front. Self-serve all the way. No talk-
to-sales until we have $30K MRR. AI is bundled because Manychat's $29/mo
AI add-on is their bleeding wound; we close that wound on day one.

Soft-cap behavior on reply limits: when a tenant exceeds their cap,
follow-on replies are routed to Haiku-only mode with a notification.
Never a hard cutoff. Never a surprise overage bill.

## North star

> **Number of drops successfully run by LaunchWings customers in the last
> 30 days, weighted by attributed DM-to-checkout revenue.**

Not signups. Not posts published. Not DMs sent. Drops that ran, drove
purchases through Shopify, and the founder approved >50% of our drafts
unchanged.

## What we are not

- We are not a generic chatbot platform. Manychat is. We are not them.
- We are not a content scheduler. Predis / Buffer / Later are.
- We are not a CRM. HighLevel is.
- We are not an email/SMS replacement. Klaviyo and Postscript are.
- We are not a Shopify mobile-app builder. Tapcart is.
- We are not a sales-team automation. SDR-AI is dead anyway.

We are the AI that runs the launch — in the founder's voice, on Instagram
and Facebook, end-to-end — and the moat is the months of edits we learn
from. Nothing more.
