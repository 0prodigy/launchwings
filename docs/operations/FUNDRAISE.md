## The arc

We pitch the **expansion story** (Klaviyo-shaped). Not the boring
acquihire pitch; not the platform-agentic moonshot.

### Today (pre-seed, $0-30K MRR)
$79-249/mo SMB self-serve. IG launch concierge for streetwear and
capsule-fashion brands. 10 paying customers in 90 days. Brand-voice +
launch-playbook moat measurable via edit-rate trend and NRR.

### Year 1 ($300K-500K MRR)
IG owned in our vertical. Manychat refugees migrating after the March
2026 pricing trauma. Edit-history compounds — average customer edit-rate
drops 20% by day-30, 35% by day-90. Beauty + accessories vertical added.
Shopify App Store listed and top-100 in category.

### Year 2 ($1-2M MRR)
TikTok Shop integration. WhatsApp Business (LATAM). International:
Brazil + Spain (Manychat's weakest AI surfaces). Multi-brand Scale tier
hardens; 30% of revenue is multi-brand accounts. Meta Tech Partner badge
secured.

### Year 3-5 ($5-10M+ MRR, $100M ARR path)
Become the social-commerce SaaS the way Klaviyo is the email-commerce
SaaS. $100M ARR path: 35-50K accounts at $149-249/mo average. Comp
universe by then is Klaviyo, Manychat, Gorgias merged.

## Series A milestones (target month 18)

Hard gates before we go to Series A:
- 500-1000 paying customers
- $300K-500K MRR ($4-6M ARR)
- NRR 105-115%
- Burn multiple < 1.2x
- AI-touched revenue ≥ 70% of customer-attributed IG sales
- Shopify Plus Certified App status
- Meta Tech Partner badge
- Brand-voice edit-rate < 30% across cohort

If we miss > 2 of these, we either (a) bootstrap longer to hit them, or
(b) reframe as a smaller exit (Manychat or Klaviyo acquihire at $20-40M).

## Pre-seed / seed milestones (target month 6-9)

Before any fundraise outreach:
- 10 paying customers + $5K MRR + 30-day retention proven
- Brand-voice edit-rate trending down (≥ 15% reduction over the cohort)
- Zero Meta API ban incidents
- One Shopify App Store featured-app slot OR Product Hunt top-3 day

Then raise $1.5-3M pre-seed at $10-15M post for 18 months runway.
Alternative: bootstrap to $100K MRR and raise Series A directly.

## Honest signal check (per the research)

Series A investors in 2026 want for AI-native SMB SaaS:
- Logo count + ARPU + retention (not just GMV)
- NRR ≥ 110%
- Growth-rate T2D3 is dead; the 2026 bar is ~3x in years 1-2, then 2x
- Burn multiple 0.8-1.2x for AI-native (lower than traditional SaaS)
- AI-touched revenue metric ≥ 60-70%
- Outcome-based ARR mix is bonus but not required

## Risks to the fundraise narrative

1. **Manychat re-prices and bundles AI** in 2026-2027 → our $79-149 price
   advantage compresses. Defense: edit-history moat + Launch Playbook IP.
2. **Klaviyo adds IG DM** as a Composer AI feature → defenders us out
   of the email-and-IG market. Defense: be acquired or hit $30M ARR
   before that ships.
3. **Meta API regression** (rate cuts, partner-program changes) → kills
   half the category overnight. Defense: Tech Partner status by month
   12; secondary surface (WhatsApp) by year 2; SMS fallback considered if
   IG access ever becomes hostile.
4. **AI commoditization compresses our brand-voice claim** → frontier
   models become uniform; our differentiation collapses to UX + workflow.
   Defense: workflow IP (Launch Playbook) + edit-history flywheel.

## What we don't pitch

  killed.

- We do not pitch agentic / autonomous-business framing. The 2026 buyer
  is skittish on autonomy after Replit/Klarna/11x.
